## How Worldpackers works

You create a profile, pay a yearly membership and can then apply to hosts. Compared to Facebook groups, the big plus is that there are reviews and Worldpackers backs you up if something goes wrong on site.

## What to look for in hosts

Really read the reviews, especially the bad ones. Ask beforehand how many hours per week are expected, where you'll sleep and whether food is included. A good host answers those questions openly.

## Entering the US

As an EU citizen you usually enter for short stays with an ESTA. Whether volunteering in exchange for accommodation is allowed on an ESTA is a question you need to look at closely, because the US is strict about it.

## Costs

Even if you don't pay for accommodation, there's still the flight, ESTA, insurance, membership and everyday life. Food in particular is expensive in Hawaii if your host doesn't include it.
