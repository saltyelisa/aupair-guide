Tahiti is pretty much on the other side of the world. There's no direct flight from Austria or Germany, you always have at least one stopover. Sounds complicated, but it isn't once you know the routes.

## The typical routes

Most people fly via Paris and then on via the US, usually Los Angeles or San Francisco, to Papeete. Others fly via Asia or New Zealand. Which route is best for you depends on price, travel time and whether you want to go through the US, because you need an ESTA even for a layover there.

## What to watch for when booking

Families and the entry rules may ask for a return ticket or proof of onward travel. So think early about whether you book a return or a flexible ticket. Watch the baggage allowance too, especially if you're staying a year.

## How to save on your flight

Being flexible with dates helps the most. Compare different routes and don't only look at departures from your home airport, sometimes starting from another airport is a lot cheaper.
