When I tell people I lived in Tahiti and Hawaii, the first reaction is almost always: "That must have been so expensive." And yes, if you go on holiday and stay in hotels, it is. But as an au pair or volunteer the math looks completely different.

## Why it's cheaper than you think

The biggest cost when travelling is usually accommodation. As an au pair you live and eat with your family, as a volunteer you usually get a bed and often food. That removes exactly what makes Tahiti and Hawaii so expensive for tourists. As an au pair you usually get pocket money on top.

## The agency factor

Many people automatically budget agency fees for an au pair year. Without an agency, those simply disappear. Weigh that against your other costs and a year in Tahiti without an agency can end up cheaper than an agency program in a country that seems cheaper.

## What you still need to budget for

The flight is the biggest chunk, French Polynesia is just really far away. Then there's insurance, possibly entry or ESTA fees, and whatever you do in your free time. Eating out and trips are expensive on the islands, a bit of planning helps.
